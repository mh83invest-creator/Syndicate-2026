## Approved detailed visual build specification — updated 31 October 2026

This section records the user-supplied next-round build plan in greater detail. It supplements the original scope and overrides any less-specific phase description above. **It is a development specification, not an implementation claim.**

### v0.9A — Front-end and strategic visual pass

Deliverables:
- New **Syndicate 2026** title-screen presentation and stronger visual identity, respecting original game's character.
- Cleaner main menu layout and background treatment; better panel borders, frames, contrast and modernised colour treatment.
- Sharper fonts/mission text where technically feasible; avoid assuming the original graphics support arbitrary font replacements.
- Polished cursor, selection highlighting and focus indication without changing input or edge-scroll semantics.
- Consistent styling across company configuration, world map and tactical mission briefing; improve briefing map frame and text readability.
- Conservative cleanup of menu/strategic presentation without wholesale UI replacement.

Acceptance: clearly visible improvement over v0.7; all original menus and transitions functional, original input hitboxes/camera untouched, responsiveness retained and all three established strategic 30 FPS update paths preserved. Verify screenshots and actual Windows navigation; compilation alone does not satisfy acceptance.

### v0.9B.1 — Night mission prototype

- Select **2–3 showcase missions**, subject to inspecting mission data/rendering paths, and make them **demonstrably night-time** rather than merely reducing global brightness.
- Primary light sources: **streetlights and their road/pavement pools**, **vehicle headlights**, **illuminated building windows**, and **neon signage**.
- Prioritise road/pavement contrast, meaningful bright and dark regions, brighter windows, headlight emphasis and neon colour accents. Light sources should guide the player's eye without changing gameplay visibility rules.
- First-pass approach to evaluate: darker *mission-specific* environmental palette, localised bright spots, emissive-looking window treatment, neon accents, visible headlight glow, contrasting illuminated roads and darker surroundings. These are desired effects subject to actual renderer capability and source/asset inspection, **not yet selected technical implementations**.
- Mood goal: unmistakably urban night, dangerous, cinematic and alive; units, roads and interactive spaces remain readable.

Acceptance: 2–3 actual missions are recognisably night-time in captured game frames; individual lighting sources visibly contribute, legibility and gameplay remain intact; no disruptive performance, input, camera, sound or simulation changes. Require Windows playtests and comparisons with unmodified daytime missions.

### v0.9B.2 — Atmosphere expansion (only after prototype acceptance)

- Expand to additional missions if prototype results are verified and acceptable.
- Introduce mission atmosphere *categories* where supported: **Day**, **Dusk**, **Night**, **Heavy urban neon night**, **Industrial night**.
- Refine consistency across mission classes without indiscriminately changing all environments.
- Evaluate optional tier-3 details only where safe and supported: stronger headlight cones, subtle animated neon flicker, varied window glow, streetlight pools and per-district tinting.
- Secondary atmosphere candidates (not unconditional requirements): emergency lights, industrial glow/haze, traffic signals, billboard lighting.
- Do not claim category selection, flicker, haze, cones or new lighting tech exists until implemented and verified.

Acceptance: designated categories visibly distinguishable where implemented, preserved daytime scenes, readable selection and routes, safe performance, and no regression to gameplay/audio/timing/camera.
