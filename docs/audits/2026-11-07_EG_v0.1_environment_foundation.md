## Technical design decision: EG-v0.1 first prototype boundary
1. **Environment before effects.** Determine how an original environment face is sourced and drawn, including ordering and palette. Do not add atmospheric glow or dynamic lighting while EG-v0.1 is under development.
2. **No guessed map categories.** Audit says certain block entries and descriptor offsets exist, not that a numbered record is a road or building facade. Semantic mapping must come from original art cross-checked against a known location in the user's first mission.
3. **Protect authored gameplay.** Do not alter map columns, road networks, buildings' bounding boxes, pedestrian/vehicle collision, mission IDs, terrain height, occlusion or mission behaviour. If replacing artwork would require such changes, stop and redesign asset treatment.
4. **Visual source fidelity + 2026 quality.** Target more believable architectural materials, sharper roof/facade and pavement definition, controlled detail density at tactical scale, coherent cyberpunk industrial palette. Original location silhouettes and readable pathways remain.
5. **First acceptance scene.** Use a screenshot/reference from the successful first-mission gameplay session at fixed window settings. The exact face record(s) must be proven against actual original data and draw path; no specific mission ID, map coordinate, block number or scene is assumed here.
6. **Prototype implementation choice pending technical gate.** Candidate options: non-destructive asset replacement of a demonstrated environment face, or a separate environment-only render enhancement pass. Select only after source-to-private-asset evidence; do not silently change entire indexed palette or screen output.
7. **Visual proof.** Paired original/prototype screenshots same scene/camera/mode, map seams and draw order, palette and UI integrity, scrolling and frame pacing, then actual Windows run. No release until passed.
